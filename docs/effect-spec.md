# Effect specification

## Medium

The final image should feel like watercolor and ink on textured paper:

- broad transparent washes for atmosphere
- layered local color for subjects
- slightly irregular ink outlines
- visible paper grain
- soft bleed at edges
- no pure black or pure white

## Color

Use a four-act color arc:

1. Warm memory: gold, ochre, coral, soft cream.
2. Conflict or storm: indigo, teal, slate, cold foam.
3. Urban exhaustion: gray blue, muted violet, dim amber.
4. Dawn resolution: pale rose, warm gold, gentle turquoise.

Avoid neon highlights except where the story explicitly needs a small accent.

## Line

- Main subject outline: 1–2 px equivalent, dark ink blue.
- Background contour: lighter and drier.
- Let the line boil slightly every 2–4 frames.
- Keep shape stability; boiling must not make anatomy jump.

## Motion

Every shot needs at least one living motion:

- breathing
- wind
- rain
- water ripples
- cloth
- hair
- light shift
- dust
- paper texture drift

Important animation principles:

- anticipation before action
- clear pose extremes
- overlap and follow-through
- weight shift
- resistance in wind and rain
- readable silhouette

## Camera

Use 2D camera moves only:

- push
- pull
- pan
- shake tied to music accents
- masked reveal
- brush wipe
- iris

Do not rotate forms through artificial 3D perspective.

## Character fidelity

Maintain one model:

- head ratio
- eye spacing
- nose and mouth placement
- hand size
- shoe size
- garment seams
- age markers
- color identity
- posture

For close-ups, check the face against the model before polishing details.

## Weather

Rain:

- slanted strokes
- varied speed and length
- droplets on brims and cloth
- wet highlights
- splash marks
- wind direction consistent within a shot

Water:

- distant pale band
- midground directional waves
- foreground foam
- highlights following wave rhythm
